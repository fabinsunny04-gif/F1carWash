Got Questions?

What types of washing services do you offer?

We provide comprehensive car care including Standard Car Washes, Premium Exterior Washes, and thorough Interior Detail Washes to keep your vehicle looking brand new.

What types of vehicles do you service?

We specialize exclusively in four-wheelers. From compact sedans to premium luxury cars and heavy-duty SUVs, our team has the specialized equipment to detail them perfectly.

How long does a full car wash take?

True to our F1 name, a standard car wash takes about 30-40 minutes. A premium exterior wash with interior detailing takes 1-2 hours.

Do you use waterless or foam wash?

We use premium thick snow foam washes for thorough cleaning without scratching your paint, followed by microfiber drying.

Is F1 Car Wash open on Sundays?

Yes, we are open on Sundays from 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM to serve you over the weekend.

Where exactly is F1 Car Wash located?

We are conveniently located in Nedumbassery, just 5 minutes away from Cochin International Airport.
